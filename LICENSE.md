### High-Voltage Electrical Infrastructure Disclaimer
The audio and visual recordings analyzed in this project were captured beneath 345 kV high-tension overhead transmission lines from public rights-of-way. **Do not trespass on utility property or attempt to approach, climb, touch, or inspect high-voltage electrical equipment.** High-voltage electrical infrastructure generates lethal electromagnetic fields and lethal arcing hazards. This repository is an acoustic and digital signal processing forensic study, not electrical engineering or safety guidance.
