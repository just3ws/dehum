# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct & Craftsmanship Charter

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and scientifically rigorous community, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **`just3ws/dehum`** project and **Panoramic View Labs (PVL)** a harassment-free and intellectually honest experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## The Craftsmanship Addendum: Physical Ground Truth & Intellectual Honesty

In addition to standard behavioral covenants, this project operates under the **Chicago Software Craftsmanship & Sovereign Verification Principles**:

1. **Physical Reality Over Simulation**: We welcome constructive debate, rigorous peer review, and mathematical critique. When a disagreement arises between an AI model's assertion and physical ground truth, physical ground truth always wins.
2. **Humility & The Beginner's Mind (*Shoshin*)**: We openly acknowledge that *"Maybe this all sucks? Maybe it's nothing."* Nobody is penalized or mocked for admitting an assumption was wrong, an equation was flawed, or a filter sounded terrible.
3. **No Automated Slop**: Contributors must listen to their own audio, inspect their own frames, and verify their outputs before asking others to review them.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub Issues or contact channels on [just3ws.com](https://www.just3ws.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
