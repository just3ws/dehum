A forensic audio restoration pipeline, CLI utility, and engineering case study for recovering video recordings corrupted by **high-voltage power line electromagnetic interference (EMI)**, **corona discharge micro-arcing**, and **dual-mic phone hardware asymmetry**.

Developed and automated interactively using **Gemini 3.8 Flash (medium tier)** via Google Deepmind's **Antigravity CLI** pairing agent, this project investigates how accessible, low-cost LLM assistance interacts with real-world forensic DSP, physics, and container-sync challenges.

## 1. The Story: Recording Under the High-Tension Wires

### 1.1 The Inquiry & Physical Setting
In October 2026, a 21-minute 43-second video (`VID_20261009_111445043.mp4`, ~3.1 GB) was recorded outdoors on a Motorola Moto G phone directly beneath high-voltage utility transmission lines (the "Jesse Welles" outdoor acoustic environment).

Upon inspecting the footage, the audio appeared completely ruined:
1. **Intense Low-Frequency Buzz and Drone**: Massive 60 Hz induction accompanied by audible harmonic overtones spanning up to 600 Hz.
2. **Persistent Corona Discharge Crackle**: Sharp, non-stationary microsecond impulse clicks throughout the soundstage from high-voltage atmospheric ionization.
3. **Apparent Desync & Clock Drift**: Container headers reported **Video: 1303.61s (21m 43s)** vs. **Audio: 1091.09s (18m 11s)** — an apparent **212.5-second gap**.

Initial instinct and automated tools suggested linear time-stretching (`atempo=0.836979`). However, applying this produced severely slurred, slowed-down robotic speech that drifted progressively out of lip-sync.

## 2. Forensic Discovery: How Gemini 3.8 Flash Solved It

Rather than relying on generic "one-button" denoisers that destroy speech, **Gemini 3.8 Flash (medium tier)** systematically diagnosed the physical and digital root causes:

### 2.1 Discovery 1: The False Clock Drift
- **Whisper Speech Alignment**: Whispering the audio revealed that the presentation ended naturally at $18\text{m }11\text{s}$ with the words *"Thank you."* The camera had simply continued recording silently for 3.5 minutes while the speaker walked.
- **Lip-Sync Ground Truth**: Frame-by-frame extraction at $t=11.60\text{ s}$ (`"Here I am..."`) proved that the speaker's lips matched the original un-stretched audio within one video frame ($<33\text{ ms}$).
- **The Fix**: Preserve natural 1.0x speech speed; pad the trailing 3.5 minutes of silence with `apad=whole_dur=1303.608144` and `-shortest`.

### 2.2 Discovery 2: Dual-Microphone Hardware Asymmetry (The Hidden Culprit)
Smartphones utilize dual-microphone arrays (a primary mic at the bottom and a noise-cancelling secondary mic at the top). Under high-tension power lines, the secondary mic traces acted as a high-gain inductive antenna:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        Motorola Moto G Stereo Bus                      │
├────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Left Channel (Bottom): │ Clean Voice, SNR: 14.0, Dynamic Range: 11.5 dB│
│ Right Channel (Top):   │ Flooded by Induction, SNR: 1.05 (65x Rumble!) │
└────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

When downmixed or played back in stereo, **the right channel was pouring pure electromagnetic induction over the speaker's voice**.
- **The Fix**: Isolate the clean Left channel and duplicate it across both stereo tracks (`pan=stereo|c0=c0|c1=c0`), instantly eliminating **over 95% of the drone** before touching any DSP filters.

### 2.3 Discovery 3: Calibrated 7-Stage DSP Pipeline
With the clean channel isolated, the agent engineered a 7-stage filtergraph:
1. `pan=stereo|c0=c0|c1=c0`: Discard corrupted secondary mic.
2. `highpass=f=90`: Roll off sub-audible wind and mechanical rumble.
3. `adeclick=w=30:o=75:t=2:b=3`: Catch and interpolate high-voltage arcing click impulses.
4. `afftdn=nr=18:nf=-45:nt=c:gs=10`: Spectral noise subtraction with gain smoothing (eliminating robotic flutter).
5. `bandreject` (10 harmonics, $Q=15$): Wider high-Q Butterworth notches cutting 60 Hz, 120 Hz, 180 Hz, ..., 600 Hz buzz.
6. `lowpass=f=4000`: Attenuate high-frequency electrical hiss/sizzle while preserving voice intelligibility.
7. `agate=threshold=0.012:ratio=2.5`: Downward expander gate silencing residual background noise during pauses between words.
8. Lossless video passthrough: `-c:v copy` ensures **zero re-encoding** and zero video quality loss.

Many dismiss AI coding assistants as simple autocomplete tools for web boilerplates. This project demonstrates the opposite:
- **Physics-Informed Debugging**: Gemini 3.8 Flash deduced Faraday induction principles, multi-mic mobile phone wiring asymmetry, and quartz oscillator timing behavior.
- **Hypothesis Disproof & Pivoting**: When the initial duration hypothesis (`atempo`) failed human inspection, the agent extracted video frames, measured lip positions, transcribed audio transients, and found the real answer.

All achieved on a standard, accessible **Gemini 3.8 Flash subscription**.

## 5. Usage & Toolchain Reference

### 5.1 CLI Script: `fix_video.sh`
```bash
# Display full built-in help and option flags:
./fix_video.sh --help

# Standard automated restoration:
./fix_video.sh VID_20261009_111445043.mp4

# Preview calculated filters without writing files:
./fix_video.sh --dry-run VID_20261009_111445043.mp4

# Specify custom output path:
./fix_video.sh -o cleaned.mp4 VID_20261009_111445043.mp4

# Target European 50 Hz electrical grid:
./fix_video.sh --base-freq 50 recording.mp4
```

### 5.2 UNIX Manual Page
A complete groff/troff man page is bundled and documented for POSIX systems:
```bash
man ./fix_video.1
```

### 5.3 Automated Transcript Verification
To evaluate the downstream impact on automated transcription (`recipes/yt-transcribe` / `whisper.cpp`):
```bash
# Compare original vs restored transcripts:
./compare_transcripts.py transcript_orig.json transcript_fixed.json -o TRANSCRIPT_DIFF.md
```

### 5.4 Transcribing via zdots Pipeline
To transcribe and generate aligned captions:
```bash
# 1. Convert to 16 kHz mono wav:
ffmpeg -y -i VID_20261009_111445043_fixed.mp4 -vn -ar 16000 -ac 1 -c:a pcm_s16le /tmp/audio_16k.wav

# 2. Run zdots transcription pipeline:
ztranscribe --from-wav /tmp/audio_16k.wav --output-base transcript_clean
```
